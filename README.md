> **The fastest, privacy-first Chrome extension to export your Amazon order history into structured CSV/Excel spreadsheets and batch download official printable PDF invoices into a single ZIP archive.**

---

## 📌 The Problem
Amazon permanently discontinued its native **"Order History Reports"** feature. As a result:
- Individuals and small business owners have to spend hours taking manual screenshots or printing receipts one by one.
- Submitting an Amazon **"Request My Data"** privacy dump can take **days or even weeks** to process, and it does not give you formatted, printable official invoices required by CPAs and bookkeepers.

**OrderFlow Pro** solves this chore completely. It runs directly in your browser, extracting your order history into a clean, accountant-ready spreadsheet and packaging all printable receipts into a single ZIP archive in seconds.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

* **⚡ 1-Click CSV & Excel Export**: Instantly extract official Order IDs (`XXX-XXXXXXX-XXXXXXX`), order dates, full item descriptions, individual prices, and order totals.
* **📦 Bulk Invoice ZIP Packaging**: Automatically fetch and package official printable Amazon invoices into an organized ZIP archive—no more clicking "View Invoice" hundreds of times.
* **🖨️ 1-Click Print & Save-as-PDF**: Every packaged receipt includes an instant print-to-PDF button compatible with any standard browser printer dialog.
* **🔒 100% Local Browser Privacy**: Your Amazon login credentials, order records, and purchase history **NEVER** leave your machine. All parsing and ZIP generation runs 100% locally in your client browser session.
* **📅 Tax Year & Date Filters**: Effortlessly filter and export records for specific fiscal tax years (e.g., 2026, 2025) or your entire order history.

## 💡 How It Works (3 Simple Steps)

1. **Install** the extension from the [Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/orderflow-pro-amazon-orde/klfaonecopnldfgebngpldgbhgofamgf).
2. Log in to your Amazon account and navigate to **Returns & Orders**.
3. Click the **OrderFlow Pro** extension icon in your Chrome toolbar, select your desired date range, and click **Export CSV** or **Bulk Package Invoices**.

---

## 🛡️ Security & Privacy Notice

OrderFlow Pro is engineered with a strict **Privacy-First** architecture:
- We do **NOT** operate any remote backend database storing your purchase history.
- We do **NOT** have access to your Amazon password, two-factor authentication, or credit card details.
- All network interactions occur strictly between your browser and Amazon's official web endpoints under your active session.
